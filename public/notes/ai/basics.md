# Context size

The maximum amount of information—measured in tokens an AI 
model can process and "remember" at one time.

# Tokens

Tokens are the basic building blocks of text or data that an 
AI understands. They can be single characters, sub-words, or 
whole words. In English, 1 token is roughly 3 to 4 characters, or 
about 3/4 of a word.

To train a model to understand language, each token is assigned an 
ID number; these ID numbers, rather than the words or even the tokens 
themselves, are used to train the model. This tokenization of language 
significantly reduces the computational power needed to process and 
learn from the text.

There is a wide variance in the amount of text that one token can represent: 
a token can stand in for a single character, a part of a word 
(such as a suffix or prefix), a whole word or even a short multiword phrase.
